# CodeGuard programming claim-evidence research dataset

## Intended use
Research and pipeline development after expert review. Not for production model training or performance claims yet.

## Contents
66 internal synthetic examples plus 6 synthetic page-held-out examples; labels are Supported, Contradicted, Insufficient Evidence.

## Creation and review
Claims were manually templated from short official Python documentation passages and some rows were carried over from the previous synthetic candidate. ‘Source checked’ is not ‘human verified’. Every record is pending expert review.

## Limitations
Small, templated, lexical polarity pairs; no compatible public dataset included; not representative of naturally occurring AI explanations. Insufficient Evidence is evidence-relative. No model was trained.
